# Natural Earth 10 m land

- **Source:** Natural Earth `ne_10m_land`, via github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector (GeoJSON).
- **Licence:** public domain.
- **Used for:** telling a cyclone landfall from a storm entering India overland (land outside India), and marking coastal districts (within 5 km of the sea) in Phase 1F and 1H.
- **Known problems:** the Natural Earth coastline and the DataMeet district coastline differ by up to a few kilometres; a 5 km buffer absorbs it.
