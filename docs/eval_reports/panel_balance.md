- 2017-06-30: First quarter after the gap (September 2016 to March 2017 are not available). - 2023-03-31: BSR-7 discontinued after December 2022; from March 2023 the district statement is built from BSR-1 and BSR-2. Only March figures are available from here (they include RRBs); the June, September and December quarters need a DBIE login and are excluded.
