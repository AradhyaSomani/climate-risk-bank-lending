# Phase 0 E: event list and power check

Generated by `python -m cri.events.power` from `configs/power.yaml`. It is blind to
post-event outcomes. Every estimate uses pseudo impact quarters whose window (-8 to +3)
ends before the real one. No district contributes quarters from the impact of any
catalogue event that treated or excluded it to 12 quarters after. The method is in the
module docstring.

Two completeness rules are compared (`rules` in the config):

- **drafted**: as drafted: a figure in every quarter, and series-break quarters (June 2017, March 2023) count as missing.
- **proposed**: series-break quarters are kept as data (decision log, 2026-11-11), and the quarters RBI did not publish for any district (September 2016 to March 2017, and the non-March quarters from June 2023) are skipped instead of failing every district. Any other missing quarter still fails the district.

## Minimum detectable effects per event

The pre-registered test is two-sided randomization inference (RI) at 5%: treatment reassigned among the event's eligible districts of the same states, 199 reassignments per pseudo window, on the mean of quarters 0 to 3. The MDE is the smallest effect with 80% power, against a fall or a rise. An event passes if its MDE is 10% or less (pre-registration section 6). Two checks sit next to it:

- **RI, all districts**: treatment reassigned among all eligible districts in India, for events whose states have few districts outside the exclusion zone.
- **Placebo dates**: the real treated set's own estimates at the pseudo dates, where the true effect is zero. Their root mean square (bias and noise together) gives a normal-approximation MDE. *Rejections* is the share of pseudo windows where the RI test rejects the real set with no effect. It should be near 5%; above that, the RI test is too optimistic for this set.

The **largest** of the three MDEs is the honest one to plan with.
