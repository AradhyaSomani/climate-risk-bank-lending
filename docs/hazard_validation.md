# Hazard score validation (Phase 2C)

Generated by `python -m cri.hazard.validation`. Every check is reported, including weak and pending ones. Thresholds, weights and cut-offs are the placeholders in `configs/hazard.yaml`.

## Summary

- Flood: built before 2012 and tested 2012 on, the composite ranks later-hit districts well (AUC 0.90, weighted) and slightly beats the single indicator `flood_events_per_decade` (AUC 0.89). On how often districts are hit (Spearman with the later hit count) the single indicator is better (0.78 against 0.73): the extra indicators add severity and terrain, which separate hit from not hit but not frequent from occasional.
- Cyclone: built before 2005 and tested 2005 on, the composite ranks later-hit districts well (AUC 0.92, pca) and matches the single indicator `cyclone_storms_per_decade` (AUC 0.92). On how often districts are hit (Spearman with the later hit count) the single indicator is better (0.77 against 0.61): the extra indicators add severity and terrain, which separate hit from not hit but not frequent from occasional.
- Kerala 2018 (section 2) is the weak check: the score was built without the event, and only the low-lying districts (Alappuzha, Thrissur, Kottayam) come out high. Idukki and Wayanad were hit mainly by landslides, which a flood-extent score cannot see. GFD observed Kerala in 24 events but never recorded Ernakulam or Pathanamthitta above 1% flooded (MODIS misses floods under cloud and canopy), so both sit near the median.
- EM-DAT, state level (section 3): a state's mean cyclone score tracks its EM-DAT cyclone records (Spearman 0.61 to 0.62 across the score versions) and damage (0.41 to 0.46). For floods the link is weak (0.15 to 0.23 for records, 0.02 to 0.11 for damage). EM-DAT counts reported disasters, which mixes hazard with exposure and reporting, and 25 of its 211 flood records name five or more states, so one monsoon season counts in states it barely touched.
- The NRSC atlas is pending on data (section 4).
- Sentinel-1 (section 5): FL-2015-CHENNAI s1_otsu against gfd: Spearman 0.12, hit IoU 0.50 over 14 districts (0.96 and 0.83 over the 7 with >= 50% mapped); FL-2018-KERALA s1_unet against s1_otsu: Spearman 0.83, hit IoU 0.67 over 8 districts (1.00 and 1.00 over the 3 with >= 50% mapped).
- Known artefact: `flood_duration_days` is high for some central Indian districts hit by one long GFD event, probably reservoirs below the JRC permanent-water threshold; it carries 5% of the weighted flood score.
